FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers for candidates and employers.

How can I apply for a vacancy?

Open Vacancies, select a job and use the Apply Now button to complete the candidate form.

Can employers submit manpower requirements?

Yes. Use the For Employers page to submit your workforce requirement.

Can I apply without seeing a listed vacancy?

You can contact the recruitment team with your profile and preferred role.

Where are your recruitment markets?

The initial site structure includes UAE, India, Nepal and Kenya. Additional markets can be added.
